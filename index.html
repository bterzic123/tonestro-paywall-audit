What this is

We went through tonestro the way a new saxophone student would, up to the paywall and past it.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and our State of Paywalls 2026 report covers 78,000+ of their paywalls. We installed tonestro, answered the onboarding, reached your paywall, tapped X to see what happens next, and read your App Store listing. Then we checked each part of the paywall against the report.

What follows is four ideas, each tied to a number from the report and each built into a clickable prototype. The prototype uses your design, not ours. Benchmarks are for Education apps in the US, your biggest App Store market; where the report only splits by category, we say so.

We did not redesign your paywall

Every element in the prototype is yours: the heavy headline, the five stars and SunnyGrace’s review, your plan cards with the purple band, your trial pill, your timeline icons and the teddy with the gift. We changed what appears and when, not how it looks.

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes first, because the report finds they win A/B tests more often than visual ones. Each idea links to its screen in the prototype.

01

Give the 7-day trial on the first paywall

Today the paywall asks €99.99 with a Continue button. Your 7-day trial only appears if someone taps X, then gets through a gift screen, “7 days free” and your “No payment now” timeline. Put the trial on Yearly, pre-selected, with that timeline on the paywall itself.

02

Name their song and instrument in the headline

Onboarding asks for the instrument, the level and a favourite song. Then the paywall says “Choose your plan” to everyone. Someone who just picked Soprano Saxophone and Bella Ciao should read that back, as in “Play Bella Ciao on your Soprano Saxophone”.

03

Show Monthly, and what Yearly saves

Monthly at €23.49 sits behind View All Plans, so €8.33 a month has nothing to look cheap next to. Both cards on screen today are yearly. Shown next to Monthly, Yearly costs 64% less.

04

Turn the close into a price offer

You already do what 96% of apps don’t: closing the paywall opens a gift. Once the trial is on the first paywall, the gift needs something new to give. Your €69.99 yearly price, already listed in the store, is 30% under €99.99.
